    About

    The house on the hill

    A family-run homestay in the Western Ghats, near Mudigere.

    Where the name comes from

    Bettada Mane is Kannada for the house on the hill. The house sits off SH-107 between Mudigere and Sakleshpura, behind a lawn edged with purple foliage, with tall trees on every side.

    How we host

    Rooms are large and kept clean. Those are the two things guests mention most often in their reviews, so we protect them first.

    The welcome is friendly. You are staying in a home. We are open 24 hours, so a late arrival is never a problem.

    Evenings are shared. Meals on the verandah and a bonfire in the trees bring groups together.
